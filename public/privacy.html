Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Karachi — City of Light collects, uses, and protects your information.

Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Password (hashed, never stored in plaintext)

Authentication and Sessions

We use secure server-side sessions with HTTP-only cookies. Your authentication credentials are never exposed to the browser or any third party. Passwords are hashed using Argon2, a modern cryptographic algorithm.

What Administrators Can See

Site administrators can only see:

  • Your email address
  • Display name
  • Account creation date
  • Last login date
  • Number of successful logins
  • Account status (active/disabled)
  • General authentication event types (login success/failure, etc.)

Administrators cannot see:

  • Your password or password hash
  • Your session cookies
  • Password reset tokens
  • Private messages or personal data

IP Address and User Agent Handling

We collect hashed IP addresses and truncated user agent summaries for authentication auditing purposes. We do not store full IP addresses. This information is retained for 90 days.

Image Sources

Images on this site are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and are subject to their respective Creative Commons licenses. We do not store images on our own servers; we link directly to verified sources.

Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with third-party services. All data is processed entirely on our servers.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data

Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact the site administrator.
